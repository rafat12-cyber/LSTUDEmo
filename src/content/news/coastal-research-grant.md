---
title: "LSTU Researchers Receive Grant for Coastal Agriculture Study"
date: 2026-10-12
type: News
summary: "A team from the Faculty of Agriculture and Marine Science will study salt-tolerant crops in the Meghna estuary."
---

A research team from the Faculty of Agriculture and Marine Science has received a competitive grant to study salt-tolerant rice and vegetable varieties for the coastal belt of Lakshmipur. The two-year project will work directly with local farmers.
