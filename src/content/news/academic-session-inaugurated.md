title: "New Academic Session Formally Inaugurated"
date: 2026-10-22
type: News
summary: "The university welcomed its newest batch of students at a colourful orientation ceremony."
---

The university formally inaugurated its new academic session at the central auditorium. The Vice-Chancellor welcomed the new students and urged them to make full use of the libraries and laboratories. Faculty deans introduced their programs, and senior students staged a cultural programme in the evening.
